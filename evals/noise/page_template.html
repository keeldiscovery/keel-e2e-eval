By environment

Each room, from quiet to as loud as the voice

Signal-to-noise ratio: 20 dB is a voice well above the room, 10 dB a noticeably loud room, 5 dB a loud one, 0 dB the noise as loud as the voice. Each cell is the share of answers recorded right, over the voices and answers in that cell.

By question type

Five controls, one spoken answer each

A yes/no, a number, a radio group, a checkbox group asking for two, and a text box spoken into. For the text box nothing is classified: the transcript is the answer, so its measure is whether the words that carry the meaning (negations, numbers, who did it) survived.
