The copilot, on a real call.

Start a source, press Float, click the card once, and run the call. Every turn is cut at 0.7 s of silence, written down by gpt-transcribe and read by Haiku against the questionnaire; the card files what it can and hands you the rest as keys. Nothing is said to the box. Wear headphones on a real call, so your microphone does not hear the other side.
