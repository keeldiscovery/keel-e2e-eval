# Spec 151 -- The copilot call bench: synthetic founder and respondent calls, three OpenAI stacks, the reliability sheet

Design of record: the founder's methodology page of 2026-11-10 (`~/Documents/projects/keel-marketing/handoff-2026-11-09/copilot/copilot-test-method.html`, artifact LbKL7ArmfrGD3J5bvhdGHH v7) with his decisions of the same day: both sides simulated; four rounds shortest first; quiet room; nothing spoken to the copilot; the heart is MAPPING a founder's own words (and own options) to Keel's questions, follow-ups tagged to the question just asked, the founder moving on by asking the next question in any order; and time to match as a first-class number. The bench decides whether there is a copilot to build and which OpenAI model carries it. **Nothing of the copilot itself is built here**; this is the referee's harness, in the shape of the noise-capture check (spec 074, `evals/noise/`), which it reuses wherever it can.

## Rules

- **FR-1 The questionnaire fixture.** `evals/copilot/questionnaire.py` reads one back-test bundle's served respondent questions (`backtest.json` -> `respondents[*].served` -> `parts[*].items[*]`: `dimension`, `question`, `control`, `options`, `escape`, `spoken{stem, optionsLine, escapeLine}`, `valueBox`, the idea's `what/how`) and writes a committed fixture `evals/copilot/fixtures/questionnaire-08-fresco.json` from `runs/20261109T195556Z-backtest-08-fresco` (the three links' items, in served order, each with a stable `id`). Controls map to the noise dataset's kinds (`gate`, `single`, `multi` with `pick`, `number`, `free`), with `value_box` -> `number` (unit from `valueBox.measure`) and `price_ladder` -> `single`. The bench is persona-agnostic: every command takes `QUESTIONNAIRE=<fixture>`; Fresco is the first instance, not a special case.
- **FR-2 The catalogue, as data.** `evals/copilot/catalogue.py` holds the founder habits and the respondent behaviours of the page, each a kind with its truth rule, no trade words in them: founder `verbatim`, `light_reword`, `options_own_words`, `options_reordered_or_missing`, `story_prompt`, `meaning_changed`, `preface`, `stacked`, `leading`, `restart`, `clarifying`, `follow_up`, `follow_ups_in_a_row`, `own_note`, `any_order`, `went_back`, `answered_for_them`, `back_channel`; respondent `straight`, `story_first`, `hedged`, `late_negation`, `number_for_yes_no`, `different_question`, `counter_question`, `interrupted`, `two_voices`. Each kind says which item kinds it applies to and the expected verdict/tier it produces, so the scorer needs no per-script judgement.
- **FR-3 The script writer.** `evals/copilot/writer.py` has a model write scripts to one fixed form: `{"id", "round", "questionnaire", "turns": [{"t": seconds, "who": "founder"|"them", "text", "silence_after": seconds, "truth": {"q": item id|null, "verdict": one of the catalogue's verdicts (as_written, same_meaning, options_own_words, meaning_changed, stacked, leading, follow_up, own_note, back_channel, nothing), "option": the expected read in the noise dataset's shapes or null, "tier": sure|likely|unsure|null, "state_after": listening|answered|follow_up|note|paused|done}}]}`. The writer is `claude-sonnet-5-5` through the Anthropic key (the writer's quality matters; its cost is cents), one call per script, the catalogue kinds for that script named in the prompt, the questionnaire's items and spoken forms given, and the output validated offline against the catalogue's rules (a `verbatim` turn equals the spoken stem; an `options_own_words` turn carries none of the option labels verbatim; a `follow_up` turn comes after an answered question; `back_channel` turns carry no question mark; the clocks are monotone). A script that fails validation is rewritten once, then dropped and counted. Rounds: **R1** one item, one founder habit, one respondent behaviour, under a minute, coverage = every applicable (item x founder habit x respondent behaviour) up to 700; **R2** one item with two or three follow-ups and their replies, then a second item, 2 to 4 minutes, about 200 drawn to cover every follow-up kind; **R3** every item of the questionnaire in the founder's own order with follow-ups and a few own-note stretches, 12 to 30 minutes, about 120; **R4** R3 run long, an item at ten minutes of follow-ups, a five-minute silence, an item never reached, 45 to 60 minutes, about 12. Seeded, so a round is reproducible. `SAMPLE=20` writes twenty scripts of a round as readable markdown to the run folder for the founder's reviewer (me) before any audio is rendered.
- **FR-4 Rendering, two tracks.** `evals/copilot/render.py` renders each script to `founder.wav` and `them.wav` on one timeline with `evals/noise/synth.tts` (gpt-4o-mini-tts, the founder's turns with an instruction for a person reading from a card and digressing, the respondent's with the noise check's instruction), silences as the script says, one voice pair by default (`VOICES=founder:them` overrides), the respondent track optionally mixed with a noise bed (`SNRS=` as the noise check, on at most a tenth of R3 by `NOISE_SHARE`). The rendered turn boundaries (start, end per turn) are written beside the audio as the truth's clocks.
- **FR-5 Three stacks behind one interface.** `evals/copilot/stacks/` : each stack takes the two tracks (or, at the text level, the script's turns) and yields `events`: `{"t_emitted", "turn", "q", "verdict", "option", "tier", "state"}` with the wall-clock time it was emitted relative to the turn's end. **Stack 1** `turn_by_turn`: turn ends from the script's silences (the browser's own silence detection stands in), `gpt-transcribe` per turn with `keywords[]` from the current item's labels and the questionnaire's stems, then `claude-haiku-5-5` (the noise pipeline's request shape: adaptive thinking, effort medium, max_tokens 1,024) on a NEW harness instruction `evals/copilot/instructions/copilot-match.md` that returns the event JSON; the respondent's answer read by the same instruction against the current item's options, `norm_out` reused. **Stack 2** `running_state`: `gpt-live-transcribe` over the Realtime websocket, both tracks, the client committing at the script's turn ends, and `claude-haiku-5-5` on `copilot-state.md` holding the questionnaire and a window of the transcript so far (`WINDOW_TURNS`, default 12). **Stack 3** `audio_direct`: `gpt-audio-mini` given each turn's audio and the questionnaire, returning the same JSON, no transcript. The text level (`LEVEL=text`) runs Stacks 1 and 2 on the script's words; Stack 3 has no text level. The instructions are the harness's own, persona-agnostic, no trade words.
- **FR-6 The scorer, offline.** `evals/copilot/score.py` scores a run's events against the scripts' truth: **A** mapping per founder turn (confusion matrix by habit kind; the `options_own_words` rows on their own); **B** answer read per item (`ok` as the noise check, by control and tier; confident-wrong); **C** follow-ups tagged to the right item with the right boundaries, own notes apart; **D** following: false moves, missed moves (needed a key), seconds behind; **E** unsure rate and honest-unsure share; **F** the limits on R4 (the line shown where the envelope says; mapping still right late); **G** time to match: question asked -> recognised, answer given -> shown, median and worst, split into turn-end wait, transcription, classification. Bars as the page (A >= 95% same-meaning and own-words options, meaning-changed >= 90%, precision >= 95%; B quiet 100% with options offered, >= 90% without, confident-wrong <= 2%; C >= 90% / boundaries >= 85%; D false move <= 2%, <= 1 key per 3 questions, <= 2 s behind; E unsure <= 10%, honest >= 80%; G <= 1.5 s / <= 2 s / worst <= 4 s), in `evals/copilot/bars.py`, the founder's to change. The sheet is one JSON and one markdown per stack per round, and a certificate line as the noise check prints it: PASS, FAIL naming the first failing cell, INCOMPLETE. Costs per stack per round from usage: OpenAI minutes at list price, Anthropic tokens at Haiku 5.5's and Sonnet 5.5's list prices.
- **FR-7 The command.** `make copilot-bench ROUND=<1..4> LEVEL=text|audio [STACKS=1,2,3] [QUESTIONNAIRE=...] [LIMIT=n] [SAMPLE=20] [RESUME=runs/<stamp>]` refuses without both `KEEL_OPENAI_API_KEY` and `KEEL_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in the shell (never logged, echoed or written), writes `runs/<stamp>-copilot-bench-r<round>-<level>/` (scripts.jsonl, sample.md, audio/, transcripts, events.jsonl, sheet.json, sheet.md, page/ with clips beside transcripts and the sheet, register-row.md) and appends a row to `runs/copilot-bench-register.md`. **PAID**: never in `make eval`, `make unit`, the matrix or a schedule; on the founder's word per round. `DRY=1` writes three scripts of the round with a fake writer, renders nothing, runs the scorer on canned events, and spends nothing.
- **FR-8 AGENTS.md.** The bench is argued in as a named paid place beside the noise-capture certificate: the script writer, the matcher instructions and the three stacks, with the same key and money rules; one paragraph, as spec 074's.
- **FR-9 Tests.** `make unit` green; `tests/test_copilot_bench.py` covers the questionnaire extraction from a stored bundle fragment, the catalogue's validation rules, the scorer on canned events for every bar (one passing and one failing row each), the certificate line, the cost arithmetic, and `DRY=1`; everything that scores runs with no key and no call. No paid run by the builder.
- **FR-10 Nothing else moves.** The noise check, the cycles, the readers, the journeys.

## Out of scope

The copilot itself (cloud, web); Level 2 in Chrome; any paid run (the founder runs the rounds on his word: R1 text then audio, then R2, R3, R4 each gated by the round before).

## Acceptance

`make unit` green on the final tree; `make copilot-bench ROUND=1 LEVEL=text DRY=1` green with no key; the builder reports the path of `sample.md` from the DRY run, the three instruction files, and any catalogue kind it could not give a mechanical validation rule to.
