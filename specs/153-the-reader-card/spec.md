# Spec 153 -- The reader card: the copilot that reads the script, records the call and digests it afterwards

Design of record: "Keel Copilot Reader Card" (`~/Documents/projects/keel-marketing/handoff-2026-11-09/copilot/copilot-reader-card.html`, artifact KgG3y9vmeFEsyuEMcUW3tY v1, 2026-11-11). The founder's decision of 2026-11-11 morning, after round 1 of the call bench (spec 151) and the listening prototype (spec 152, eval master d35eaa9): the copilot shows the questions and the options the way the AI avatar speaks them; the founder reads them and presses the option on the respondent's behalf; the box infers nothing during the call; the whole call is recorded and digested afterwards; where a pick goes against the founder the card shows a follow-up hint at once and the founder asks what they like; at the end the founder may ask their own questions, and then tells the respondent the questions are done and invites theirs, which are recorded and kept as a tagged note on the interview (their sentiment is read later, not now); a stop button ends each stretch; when the screener (the gate) fails, the card says so and, instead of the product's thank-you, offers the same free-speech stretch so the founder can still ask what they like. "Record the call, trust the founder." Measured reasons on the page: round 1's two failure classes (a three-second lag, frame words dropped silently) and its one hang per call all lived in live inference, which this design removes.

This replaces spec 152's live path and reuses its page, window, sources, run folder and tests. It is an eval-repo tool, not product code: no sign-in, no project, nothing in keel-cloud or keel-web. The wording on the card is the questionnaire's own `spoken` blocks; the follow-up hints are the product's composer's wording for the Fresco lock, carried as data.

## Rules

- **FR-1 The call runs without a model.** `evals/copilot/prototype/server.py` keeps its `ThreadingHTTPServer` on `127.0.0.1:8765` (`PORT=`), the Origin check and the plain-file routes, and loses every live model call. New routes: `POST /start` (opens a run folder, returns `{run, started_at}`), `POST /audio?who=founder|them&seq=` with a 16-bit mono WAV chunk body (the page posts each side in chunks of at most 60 s while the call runs, so a crash loses at most a minute; the server appends them, in order, into `founder.wav` and `them.wav`), `POST /key` (`{key, item, at, shown}`: the founder's filing, applied to the server's record and answered with the state the card shows), `POST /end` (closes the files, writes `session.json`). No `KEEL_OPENAI_API_KEY` or `KEEL_ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` is read by the server; `make copilot-prototype` starts without them and says so. Never a key in any file.
- **FR-2 The card reads the script.** `copilot.html/js/css` show, for the current item, the `spoken` block of the questionnaire fixture as it is: `anchor` (when present), `stem`, `countLine`, `optionsLine`, `escapeLine`, and the options numbered 1..n with `0` for the escape; a `number` item shows the stem and a typed-number field (digits, `Enter` files; `0` files the escape). A `two_steps` item shows the first step's options; pressing the first or the last band shows the fixture's `under` / `over` line with its two halves numbered, and the half is the filing. Keys: `1`-`6` file and advance; `0` the escape; `Z` undoes the last filing and shows that item again; `→`/`Space` skips (the item stays open in the progress row); `←` goes back, the filing stays until changed; `Enter` stops a stretch (FR-3, FR-4); `Esc` floats the card (Document Picture-in-Picture, 380 × 560, as 152 built it). The header shows a red dot while the call is recorded, "Question n of N" and the progress row; a **Filed** row repeats the last filing in the question's own words (number + unit + window; option text), green, with `Z` undo. Gone: `X`, `F`, `R`, `P`, `T`, the Heard row, the frame pills, the Now/Filed wait states, turn cutting, the AnalyserNode. A **Start** button begins recording both sources and shows the consent line (keel-cloud canon `report-and-follow-up-design-2026-10-17.md` §9, the text interview's wording, carried as data in the questionnaire fixture or a sibling file) for the founder to read; an **End** button posts `/end`.
- **FR-3 The follow-up.** `evals/copilot/prototype/follow-ups.json`, data not code: for each item id of the Fresco fixture, `against` (the filings that go against the founder's locked answer on that rung, derived from keel-cloud's `FollowUpComposer`, `Ladder` and the Fresco lock in `keel-cloud-docs/src/test/resources/keel/backtest-r18/08-fresco.yaml`), and `hint` (the follow-up line the composer would speak for that pick, with the pick said back as a person says it: spoken-questions guidelines §21; a `{pick}` placeholder filled from the option text where the composer does so). The builder writes the file by reading the product's code and the lock, invents no wording, and lists in tasks.md the source line of each hint. When a filing matches `against` and no follow-up has been asked this call, the card shows the hint at once ("A hint to start from · ask what you like"), starts a marked stretch (`keys.jsonl` records `follow_up_start {item, at}`), and `Enter` or the on-card **Stop** button records `follow_up_stop` and shows the next item. One per respondent, on the first rung against (D2/D3 of the follow-up design); later rungs against file and move on. `→` skips the follow-up (recorded as skipped). The founder may ask several questions inside the stretch; nothing is cut or inferred.
- **FR-4a The screener.** When the questionnaire carries a gate item (`kind: "gate"`, options Yes/No, as `evals/copilot/questionnaire.py` builds it from a served link; the Fresco commercial fixture has none), it is read first like any item. A filing of No shows the screened-out card: "They don't fit the screen. Keel's interview would end here. Ask what you like, or End.", with one button **Ask what you like** that starts a marked stretch (`screened_start`), **Stop**/`Enter` ends it (`screened_stop`), and **End** closes the call; no further item is shown and `session.json` records `screened_out: true`. A filing of Yes continues to the next item. `Z` on the screened-out card undoes the No and shows the gate again.
- **FR-4 The two closing stretches.** After the last item the card shows two buttons in order. **Ask your own questions**: pressing it (or `Enter`) starts a marked stretch (`extra_start`), the red dot and a running clock show, **Stop**/`Enter` ends it (`extra_stop`). **Their questions**: the card shows the line for the founder to say, carried as data beside the consent line ("That's all of my questions. Is there anything you'd like to ask me?"), pressing it starts a marked stretch (`theirs_start`), **Stop**/`Enter` ends it (`theirs_stop`). Either may be skipped with `→`. The card then shows "Done" with the counts filed; **End** closes the call.
- **FR-5 The log.** `runs/<ts>-copilot-reader/`: `founder.wav`, `them.wav` (one file each, the whole call), `keys.jsonl` (every key and button with its wall clock and the item showing), `filings.json` (item → `{option|value|escape, at, undone?}`), `session.json` (questionnaire path, started, ended, items filed / skipped, follow-up item or null, extra stretch or null, their-questions stretch or null, screened_out and its stretch or null, consent shown at). Who spoke is the file, not an inference.
- **FR-6 The digest.** `make copilot-digest RUN=runs/<ts>-copilot-reader` (needs `KEEL_OPENAI_API_KEY`; refuses without it, exit 2) transcribes each side's file in the stretches `keys.jsonl` marks (the follow-up, the founder's extra questions, the respondent's questions, the screened-out stretch) and, for the pick check, the respondent's file between one filing and the previous one, with `HttpClients.transcribe` as the bench's stack 1 used it (`keywords` from the item). It writes `digest.json` and `digest.md`: the follow-up as turns in time order (founder line, respondent line, …; from the two files' timestamps, no diarisation) and as the product's `followUp {dimension, itemId, text, how: "RECORDED"}` where `text` is the respondent's words of the stretch; the extra stretch as turns under "Your own questions", filed as nothing; the respondent's questions as turns under "Their questions", kept on the interview block as `notes: [{tag: "their_questions", turns}]`, filed as nothing (a sentiment reading of it is a later spec, not this one); the screened-out stretch as turns under "After the screen", kept as `notes: [{tag: "screened_out", turns}]`, the interview block carrying `screenedOut: true` and no answers past the gate; an `interview` block in the shape of `InterviewResponse` (answers per item from `filings.json`, `followUp`); and a **pick check**: for each filed choice, the respondent's words in that window and whether they name a different option (a Haiku call on the bench's `copilot-match.md` instruction, run offline, allowed here because the call is over), listed for the founder to read, never applied. Cost per digest is printed.
- **FR-7 Tests.** `tests/test_copilot_prototype.py` rewritten for this shape: the routes (start, chunked audio appends in order, every key of FR-2 against the fixture's items including the two-step halves and the typed number, follow-up shown on the first rung against only, skipped by `→`, the two closing stretches, the gate: Yes continues, No shows the screened-out card and its stretch and nothing after, `Z` restores the gate; end), the log's files and shapes, the server starting without keys, `follow-ups.json` keyed by the fixture's ids with an `against` and a `hint` per item; the digest against `FakeClients` (stretch cutting by timestamps, turns in time order from two files, `followUp` shape, pick check lists a disagreement and applies nothing, refusal without the key). The gate tests use a second, small fixture the builder writes under `evals/copilot/fixtures/` with a gate item and two items after it, in the fixture's shape, no trade word. No browser in tests; the page's JavaScript is read, not run.
- **FR-8 Nothing else.** No change to `evals/copilot/stacks/`, `score.py`, `bench.py`, `catalogue.py`, `bars.py` or `instructions/`; spec 152's files are edited in place (no second prototype folder); no new dependency beyond the venv's; no trade or company word in code (the fixture, `frames.json` if kept, and `follow-ups.json` are data); AGENTS.md's prototype paragraph rewritten for this shape, with the digest command.

## Acceptance

1. `make copilot-prototype` starts with no key in the environment and serves the page; the card shows item 1's spoken stem and its typed-number field; `1`-`6`, `0`, `Z`, `→`, `←`, `Enter`, `Esc` do what FR-2 says, checked by the route tests.
2. A filing in an item's `against` shows that item's hint at once, once per call; `Enter` ends the stretch and the next item shows; a second rung against shows no hint.
3. After item 6, **Ask your own questions** and then **Their questions** each start and **Stop** ends a marked stretch, the second showing the line to say; **End** writes `session.json`.
3b. On a fixture with a gate, No shows the screened-out card, **Ask what you like** and **Stop** mark a stretch, no later item shows, `session.json` says `screened_out: true`; Yes continues.
4. The run folder holds exactly `founder.wav`, `them.wav`, `keys.jsonl`, `filings.json`, `session.json`; the WAV files are 16-bit mono and their chunks arrive in order.
5. `make copilot-digest RUN=…` on a run folder made by the tests' fakes writes `digest.md` with the follow-up as turns and as `followUp {…, how: "RECORDED"}`, the extra stretch and the respondent's questions as turns, the interview block with its `their_questions` note, and a pick check that lists a disagreement without changing `filings.json`; without the key it refuses, exit 2.
6. The full suite green; no key in any file (`grep -rE 'sk-ant|api-key|KEY=' evals tests` finds nothing new).

## Judgement calls left to the builder

The chunk length (≤ 60 s) and WAV rate; the typed-number field's behaviour on a non-number; whether the Filed row shows the last two filings or one; the exact against sets and hints, each traced to the product's code and the lock in tasks.md. Where the product's composer needs data the fixture does not carry, the builder writes what it needs into `follow-ups.json` and says so; it does not change the fixture.
